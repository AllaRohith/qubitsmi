Engagement models

Five ways to engage. Pick the shape that fits the work.

Our engagement models are tailored to project requirements, delivery timelines and customer needs, enabling flexible and cost-effective access to engineering expertise. Not sure which one fits? Describe the work and we’ll recommend one.

Discuss your project →

  • Fixed scope

    Milestone-based. The spec is agreed up front and we commit to the deliverables for each milestone.

    Best forDefined IP, verification or bring-up milestones

  • Fixed price

    A turnkey deliverable — such as verification IP or a test suite — at a quoted price.

    Best forTurnkey work against a stable spec

  • Time & materials

    Engineering on demand, with weekly progress updates and monthly invoicing.

    Best forEvolving scope and exploratory work

  • Staff augmentation

    Senior engineers embedded in your team — your tools, your repository, your stand-up.

    Best forScaling an in-house design or DV team

  • Research collaboration

    Joint research, proofs of concept and publications in QKD, quantum error correction and post-quantum cryptography.

    Best forUniversities, labs and R&D groups
