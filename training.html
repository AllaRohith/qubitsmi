Project practice

  1. 01
    Quantum computing

    Quantum information, circuits and algorithms, learned from first principles through to simulation.

    • Build and simulate your own quantum circuits
    • Study a foundational algorithm
    • Guided Qiskit mini-project
  2. 02
    Quantum communication

    Applying quantum principles to communication, networking and security, with key distribution as the worked example.

    • Simulate a simple key-distribution workflow
    • Study communication protocols
    • Review a quantum network use case
  3. 03
    Quantum machine learning

    Combining quantum and classical methods through variational circuits and optimization.

    • Build a hybrid workflow
    • Explore a variational circuit
    • Benchmark a small quantum model against a classical one
  4. 04
    Quantum semiconductors

    How semiconductor device ideas map onto quantum hardware.

    • Review a device architecture
    • Study simulation and characterization workflows
