Leadership approach

Three disciplines. One leadership team.

The leadership team’s key differentiator is the integration of customer-focused engineering delivery, reusable technology development and research-led innovation — across both practices.

  1. 01

    Customer-focused delivery

    Engineering shaped around each customer’s specification and schedule, under the engagement model that fits — and built for long-term client relationships.

  2. 02

    Reusable technology

    Verification environments, regression automation and engineering methods developed to be reused across programmes, not rebuilt for each one.

  3. 03

    Research-led innovation

    Research roadmaps and proofs of concept where quantum technologies meet semiconductor engineering, aimed at practical applications.

VLSI practice · leadership

VLSI engineering. Technical leadership.

Leadership remit

  • Building engineering organizations and developing technical teams
  • Acquiring customers and establishing long-term client engagements
  • Defining technical strategy
  • Guiding engineering execution
  • Mentoring engineers

QubitSemi’s VLSI expertise spans IP/SoC design, ASIC and FPGA development and verification, RISC-V processors, AI/ML accelerators, Network-on-Chip (NoC) and coherency.

Technical capabilities include SystemVerilog, UVM-based verification environments, constrained-random and coverage-driven methodologies, SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA), protocol verification, coverage analysis and closure, regression automation, gate-level simulation and Cadence Palladium-based hardware-assisted verification.

Quantum practice · leadership

Quantum technologies. Research leadership.

Leadership remit

  • Defining research roadmaps
  • Developing proof-of-concept solutions
  • Guiding research initiatives
  • Exploring where quantum technologies meet semiconductor engineering

The leadership brings expertise in quantum computing, quantum cryptography, quantum error correction, quantum key distribution (QKD), quantum machine learning (QML), quantum optimization, cybersecurity and quantum hardware architectures.

This includes defining research roadmaps, developing proof-of-concept solutions, guiding research initiatives and exploring opportunities at the intersection of quantum technologies and semiconductor engineering — with the aim of translating emerging concepts into practical applications in secure communication and next-generation computing architectures.

Research areas

  • Quantum cryptography
  • Quantum error correction
  • QKD
  • QML
  • Quantum optimization
  • Cybersecurity
