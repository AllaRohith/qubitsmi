02

Functional verification

Scalable, reusable and coverage-driven verification environments using SystemVerilog, UVM, OVM, Verilog and C/C++, at IP, subsystem and full-chip SoC level — incorporating assertion-based verification, metric-driven methodologies and comprehensive functional coverage. Additional expertise includes gate-level simulation, unit-delay and SDF-based timing simulations, and DPI integration.

  • SystemVerilog
  • UVM
  • OVM
  • Verilog
  • C/C++
  • Assertion-based
  • Metric-driven
  • Functional coverage

  • Unit-delay & SDF timing
  • DPI integration
